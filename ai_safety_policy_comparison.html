🔍 Detailed Parameter Evaluation by KPI

1. Risk Threshold Specificity

  • Anthropic (9.2 / 10): Leads with its AI Safety Levels (ASL-1 through ASL-4), explicitly linking operational escalation triggers to biological, chemical, radiological, nuclear (CBRN) hazards and autonomous replication capabilities.
  • OpenAI (8.9 / 10): Employs a structured 4-domain risk matrix (Cyber, CBRN, Persuasion, and Autonomous Replication) with Low, Medium, High, and Critical capability tiers.
  • Google / DeepMind (8.7 / 10): Defines granular Critical Capability Thresholds (CCTs) covering CBRN, cyber operations, and manipulation/persuasion capabilities.

    • 2. Pause Commitment Bindingness

  • Anthropic (8.8 / 10): Maintains an explicit, non-optional halt commitment on model training and deployment if ASL-3 capability thresholds are reached prior to containment safeguards being implemented.
  • OpenAI (8.6 / 10): Enforces a policy requiring development/deployment pauses if a model reaches a pre-mitigation rating of "Critical" until risk levels are mitigated back to "High" or lower.
  • Google / DeepMind (8.5 / 10): Uses multi-stage gated safety review gates prior to major model training and deployment milestones.

    • 3. External Governance Independence

  • Anthropic (9.5 / 10): Industry-leading governance structure featuring the Long-Term Benefit Trust, a independent corporate body holding ultimate legal authority and board appointment/veto power over commercial priorities.
  • OpenAI (7.9 / 10): Governed by a Board-level Safety & Security Committee alongside executive oversight; oversight remains primarily internal to board governance.
  • Google / DeepMind (7.8 / 10): Rely on an internal Responsible AI Council, supplemented by specialized external advisory boards and corporate risk committees.

    • 4. Red-Teaming Coverage

  • Google / DeepMind (9.3 / 10): Combines the dedicated DeepMind AI Safety research unit with extensive infrastructure-level red-teaming via the Secure AI Framework (SAIF).
  • Anthropic (9.0 / 10): Integrates Constitutional AI automated alignment techniques with mandatory independent third-party external red-teaming audits prior to release.
  • OpenAI (8.9 / 10): Operates an External Red Teaming Network comprised of academic, security, and policy domain experts performing stress tests across capabilities.

    • 5. Public Transparency Score

  • Google / DeepMind (9.1 / 10): High level of technical open-sourcing, whitepaper disclosures, frontier safety research, and broad publication of safety evaluations.
  • Anthropic (8.7 / 10): Publishes its full RSP policy document online alongside threat model benchmarks and ASL definitions.
  • OpenAI (8.4 / 10): Publishes System Cards, Preparedness scorecards, and Model Specs detailing safety interventions and evaluation findings.
